# Skull King — The Rascal's Scoring

> Transcribed from `skull-king-rascal-scoring.pdf` (Grandpa Beck's Games). An alternate Scoring Mode to [rulebook.md](rulebook.md)'s Traditional Scoring — see the domain glossary in `CONTEXT.md` for how the two modes relate.

This scoring method is for players who want a more even-keeled game: **every player has the same potential score each round, regardless of their bid.**

## Potential Score

The potential score for a round equals **10 points per card dealt that round** — the same for every player, no matter what they bid.

```
Example: if 5 cards are dealt, the potential score is 50 points,
whether a player bid 0, 1, 3, or 5.
```

How much of that potential a player actually earns depends on how close their bid was to the tricks they took, expressed as one of three **Outcomes**:

| Outcome           | Condition                             | Share of potential round points earned |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Direct Hit**    | Took exactly the number of tricks bid | All (100%)                             |
| **Glancing Blow** | Off by 1                              | Half (50%)                             |
| **Complete Miss** | Off by 2 or more                      | None (0%)                              |

## Bonus Points

Bonus points (see `rulebook.md`'s Bonus Points section for what earns a bonus) follow the **same Outcome-based split** as round points:

| Outcome       | Share of potential bonus points earned |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Direct Hit    | All                                    |
| Glancing Blow | Half                                   |
| Complete Miss | None                                   |

## Worked Examples

```
Example A: 3 cards dealt (potential = 30 points).
Harry, Shirley, and Poe bid 0, 1, and 2 respectively. All three hit their
bid exactly (Direct Hit) → each scores 30 points.

Example B: 4 cards dealt (potential = 40 points).
Harry bids 1 and takes 1  → Direct Hit    → +40 points
Shirley bids 0 and takes 1 → off by 1, Glancing Blow → +20 points
Poe bids 4 and takes 2    → off by 2, Complete Miss  → +0 points
```
